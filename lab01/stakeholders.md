# Stakeholder Analysis: Realtime Quiz Project

## Priority Stakeholders (High Influence, High Interest)

The two primary stakeholders exhibiting both high influence and high interest are **S01 (Head of the IT department, Sponsor)** and **S02 (Supervising Lecturer, Project Manager)**. They demand the PM's immediate and continuous attention because they govern the project's strategic mandate, resource allocation, and day-to-day execution. The Sponsor holds executive authority over project continuation, budget authorization (200 USD cap), and milestone sign-offs at the week-7 MVP demo and week-14 pilot gates. The Project Manager oversees sprint execution, technical direction, risk mitigation, and team performance across the 490 person-hours. Keeping these two stakeholders aligned ensures continuous organizational support, rapid escalation resolution, and formal authorization across all project lifecycle stages.

## Veto Stakeholder

The critical veto stakeholder is **S08 (Data protection officer)**, who possesses high institutional influence despite low day-to-day interest, because they hold the regulatory authority to prohibit the collection and storage of student records and unilaterally cancel the week-14 live classroom pilot if campus data privacy and retention criteria are not formally approved in advance.
