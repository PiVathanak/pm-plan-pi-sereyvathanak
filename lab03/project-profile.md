## Profile summary

Realtime Quiz is a hybrid-leaning project: its scope is fixed on paper (eight Must features) but its technical risk and its early-feedback milestones (week 7 MVP, week 14 pilot) are high. The two dominant criteria are requirements stability (weight 40, pulling toward predictive) and technical uncertainty (weight 20, pulling toward adaptive). Task 2 must therefore include a hybrid approach among its candidates, alongside one fully predictive and one fully agile option for comparison.
